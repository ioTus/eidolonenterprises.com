Eidolon Enterprises is a software company in Portland, Oregon. It publishes TrackBack, Eatezy, and GitBridge MCP, and stewards IME, an open convention for telling the AI tools you use who you are and how you work. Formed in 2021.

Products

TrackBack
A voice-first symptom investigation app for iOS. Speak about your day, and it finds the patterns behind recurring health issues.
Eatezy
Say what you cannot eat in your own words. It reads a menu, a label, or a dish and tells you what you can.
GitBridge MCP
An open-source MCP server that connects AI assistants to GitHub repositories. MIT licensed.
openIME
The open convention behind IME: your identity and working rules as plain Markdown in a repo you own, read by every AI you use.
