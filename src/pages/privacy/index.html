Privacy

Effective September 17, 2026

This website

eidolonenterprises.com is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and makes no requests to third-party services; its fonts are served from this domain. The hosting provider keeps standard web server access logs, which record the requesting address, the page requested, and the time of the request.

TrackBack

TrackBack is local-first. What you record stays on your device, and the free tier requires no account.

Eatezy

Eatezy reads the menus, labels, and dishes you photograph in order to tell you what you can eat. Its full privacy policy will be published on this page before the app is released.

GitBridge MCP and openIME

GitBridge MCP and openIME are open-source software that you run in your own environment. Eidolon Enterprises does not receive data from your use of them.
